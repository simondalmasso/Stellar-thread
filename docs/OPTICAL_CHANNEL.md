# Optical Channel

The optical adapter is design-only. It requires a professional observatory, laser safety officer, aircraft and satellite coordination, atmospheric constraints, pointing verification, wavelength/pulse plan, shutter interlocks and compatible receiver. The public app cannot actuate a laser.

LaserSETI informed the listening model: short monochromatic optical flashes can be searched as technosignatures, but detection must still pass instrument, satellite, aircraft, atmospheric and independent-observation checks.

## Estado de verdad

Este documento distingue datos oficiales, derivados, observaciones auténticas grabadas, pruebas sintéticas, revisión humana y datos no disponibles. Ningún pico, score o coincidencia se presenta como mensaje extraterrestre.
