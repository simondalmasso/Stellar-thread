# Regulatory

Argentina: ENACOM defines radioamateur licensing, bands, categories, technical conditions and maximum powers. Internationally, ITU Radio Regulations require stations not to cause harmful interference. Facility operators remain responsible for exact current rules, protected bands, identification and coordination.

Optical transmission additionally requires laser, airspace and satellite safety controls. This repository provides preparation software, not legal authorization.
